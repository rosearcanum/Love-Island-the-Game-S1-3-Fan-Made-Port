In app > manifests > AndroidManifest.xml, find the line that starts with

    <activity
        android:name=".MainActivity"

and add these two lines inside that <activity ...> tag (right under android:name):

        android:screenOrientation="portrait"
        android:configChanges="orientation|screenSize|screenLayout|smallestScreenSize|keyboardHidden"

That keeps the game upright like the original app. Don't add any internet
permission: the app works fully offline.
